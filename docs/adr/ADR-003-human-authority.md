# ADR-003: Human Retains Reply and Disposition Authority

**Status:** Accepted for V1

## Context

The application exists to help allocate attention, not impersonate the author or decide which readers matter.

## Decision

AI may classify, cluster, summarize, explain, and suggest priority. The human decides whether to inspect, reply, investigate, propagate, or take no action. V1 does not autonomously publish substantive replies or perform moderation actions.

## Consequences

- Automation stops before consequential social action.
- Suggested drafts may be considered later but require explicit approval.
- Evaluation focuses on attention support rather than autonomous engagement volume.
