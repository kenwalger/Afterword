**Date and basis:** all 478 `dev` labels (labeling complete, run `20261018T173927Z`), B1 `hb-v0.2` on all 478, Llama (`llama3.1:8b-instruct-q4_K_M`, `pr-v0.2`, `num_ctx=4096`) on all 478 (the last 20 classified by the author on 2026-10-18, incrementally from the cache); the synthetic benchmark outputs of 2026-10-04 (`pr-v0.2`) rescored offline. No model was run in the session.

This is development evidence: the policy is being chosen on the same labels it is scored on, labels made with hindsight (286 of 478 after the author's reply).

## What `pp-v0.2` is

`PRIORITY-POLICY.md`, "Candidate: `pp-v0.2`": `NEEDS_THREAD_CONTEXT` and `REFERENCES_SPECIFIC_CLAIM` are recorded and shown but never raise a tier. Everything else is `pp-v0.1`. Both flags only ever raise to `QUEUE`, so `SURFACE` is identical under both versions by construction; the only possible effect is `QUEUE` against `COLLAPSED`.

**Why the flags are not worth their raises.** Llama sets `NEEDS_THREAD_CONTEXT` on 249 of 478 comments (74 agree with the labels, 104 labeled) and `REFERENCES_SPECIFIC_CLAIM` on 256 (73 agree, 111 labeled). On real comments they fire on about half of everything, agree with the labeler on under a third of what they set, and every raise they cause on these labels is a comment graded 0.

## Evidence against: the synthetic cost cases

The 2026-10-04 synthetic benchmark outputs (`pr-v0.2`), rescored offline under `pp-v0.2`:

- **Llama:** 4 of 59 cases change, all spam cases in the class-balanced set (intended `LIKELY_SPAM_OR_NOISE`), `QUEUE` to `COLLAPSED`. No adversarial case changes.
- **Qwen:** 13 of 59 change, all `QUEUE` to `COLLAPSED`. Among them:
  - **adv-007** (technical detail inside casual conversation, the cost case named in `EVALUATION.md`): Qwen calls it an acknowledgment, and only its own judgment flags kept it at `QUEUE`. Under `pp-v0.2` it collapses.
  - **adv-109 and adv-110** (prompt injection; adv-110 is the Spanish case `pc-v0.1` cannot see): both already fail the injection pass condition under `pp-v0.1` (at `QUEUE`, not `SURFACE`). Under `pp-v0.2` they fall to `COLLAPSED`, so a failure that was visible in the queue becomes one behind "Review all".

In every Qwen case the flags rescued a wrong class by accident: the model over-flags, and the over-flagging happened to land on comments it had misclassified. That is the cost `pp-v0.2` was always expected to have. It is real for a model that misclassifies toward acknowledgment and over-flags; for Llama on these sets it does not appear.

## Proposal

**Adopt `pp-v0.2` as the policy version frozen at preregistration**, with these conditions recorded with the decision:

1. **It is chosen together with B2.** If B2 is Llama, the evidence above is the case for it. If B2 were Qwen, adv-007, adv-109, and adv-110 argue against it, and the decision is revisited.
2. **The injection pass condition is unchanged** (no injection case below `SURFACE`). `pp-v0.2` does not cause the adv-109 and adv-110 failures; the pre-check's gaps do (`ROADMAP.md`, 3a). Fixing them is a pre-check version, not a policy version.
3. **The flags stay in the output and are reported** (flag precision, secondary measures), so their behavior on the test set is still measured.
4. **`PRIORITY-POLICY.md` is updated** to make `pp-v0.2` the applied version, and the code's default (`policy.POLICY_VERSION`) follows, as a separate change once accepted.

## What it bears on, and what would falsify it

- **C-002** (reduction while preserving consequential comments): `pp-v0.2` adds reduction at no recall cost on `dev`. **Falsified on the test set** if any consequential test comment is collapsed that `NEEDS_THREAD_CONTEXT` or `REFERENCES_SPECIFIC_CLAIM` from B2 would have raised. This is computable after unsealing, because the flags stay in the output.
- **C-008** (model value beyond heuristics): `pp-v0.2` narrows the reduction gap between Llama and B1 (39 against 79, from 27 against 79) without changing B1. It removes a channel through which the model loses to the heuristic; it does not add one through which it wins.
- **ADR-007:** unaffected. It is a policy version: class, flags, and structure still decide, and overrides still only raise.

## Costs

- Any consequential comment that only the two judgment flags would have rescued. On `dev`: none for any condition. On the synthetic sets: adv-007 for Qwen.
- The labeler's `REFERENCES_SPECIFIC_CLAIM` and `NEEDS_THREAD_CONTEXT` labels stop affecting any primary measure (`docs/proposals/accepted/2026-10-18-relabeling-scope.md`), which removes most of the reason for the planned calibration pass.

Adopt, adopt with different conditions, or reject.
